Before you book

The usual questions.

Do I have to be ready to buy right now?

No. Plenty of the people I speak to are three to six months away. Knowing what to fix in your finance, your search area or your offer strategy before you're in the market is usually worth more than doing it in a rush afterwards.

What does a buyer's agent actually cost?

Fees depend on the service and the price bracket, and I'll walk you through the numbers plainly on the call — before you commit to anything. The 15-minute consultation itself is free.

Do you work with first-home buyers?

Yes. First-home buyers are often the ones losing the most offers, because they're competing against people who've done this three or four times before.

Which parts of Perth do you cover?

Perth metro — coastal, river and hills corridors. If your search area sits outside where I can genuinely add value, I'll say so on the call and point you somewhere better.

Is this a sales call?

It's a conversation about your situation. If a buyer's agent isn't the right answer for you, that's a perfectly good outcome for fifteen minutes.
